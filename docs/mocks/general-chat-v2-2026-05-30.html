How does the live event stream get from the orchestrator to the dashboard?
pi
Thought for 6s

The chain is append-only EventStore → LiveEventStore (assigns a monotonic sequence) → Fastify SSE route → Next proxy that passes the body through untouched → client reducer keyed by envelope id.

I should emphasize that the sequence number is what makes last-event-id resume work, and that the proxy must not buffer.

The stream flows through four hops, each with one job:

  1. LiveEventStore assigns every envelope a monotonic sequence and fans it out to subscribers.
  2. The Fastify route at routes/live.ts writes them as SSE frames (id: <sequence>) and emits a heartbeat every 25s.
  3. The Next proxy streams the upstream body verbatim with x-accel-buffering: no so nothing is held back
